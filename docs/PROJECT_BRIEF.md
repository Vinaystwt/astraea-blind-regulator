# Project Brief

Astraea lets tokenized fund issuers prove subscription compliance onchain without exposing investor amounts or outcomes.

## Hook

RWA issuers need to prove compliance without doxxing investor amounts. Astraea keeps investor facts encrypted, computes the rule onchain, and emits public receipts without revealing private outcomes.

## Pitch

Public chains are bad for regulated finance because proving compliance usually means exposing investor data. Astraea fixes that with Zama FHEVM. Investors submit encrypted subscription amounts, the contract checks both the per-investor maximum and the evolving fund exposure capacity while the data stays encrypted, and Sepolia records a public compliance receipt. The public sees that the rule was evaluated, the investor decrypts their own result, and the regulator decrypts only the aggregate report. Astraea is audit without surveillance.

## APAC Framing

APAC capital markets are fragmented across jurisdictions, investor categories, and cross-border fund distribution rules. Astraea shows how tokenized funds can publish verifiable onchain compliance receipts without exposing investor books on a public chain.
